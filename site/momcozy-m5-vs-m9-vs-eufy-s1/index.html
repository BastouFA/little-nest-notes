How to pick in 30 seconds

  • You want the safe, simple choice: Momcozy M5. It's the one Babylist's hands-on comparison singled out as the quietest and most discreet Momcozy.
  • You like fine-tuning, or your two sides need different settings: Momcozy M9, but buy where returns are easy and register the warranty.
  • Comfort is your top priority and you pump near an outlet: eufy S1.
  • You pump at work or travel and can't recharge between sessions: eufy S1 Pro, for the charging case.

Momcozy M5

  • 9 levels · 280 mmHg
  • 4 oz cups
  • No app
  • ~$120 / $200

The M5 is Momcozy's best-known wearable for a reason: it's low-profile, quiet and easy to use with on-device buttons only. Reviewers measured roughly 100–120 minutes of pumping per charge, and it takes about two hours to recharge. You can't pump while it's charging, and the parts are hand-wash only.

Why parents pick it

  • Quiet and discreet
  • Simple, no app needed
  • Longest track record of the four

Trade-offs

  • Fewer suction levels
  • Can't pump while charging
  • Hand-wash parts

Momcozy M9

  • 15 levels · 300 mmHg
  • App control
  • Left/right separate

The M9 is the M5's more adjustable sibling: more suction levels, slightly higher maximum suction, app control, and the ability to set each side separately, which helps if one side responds differently. Mid-pack noise (one review measured around 46 dB) and a milk level that's hard to see while pumping are the small complaints. The bigger one: several Babylist reviewers in February and March 2026 reported motors failing within about a month, and a replacement that wouldn't charge.

Why parents pick it

  • Most adjustable of the four
  • Separate left and right settings
  • Often discounted

Trade-offs

  • Recent durability complaints
  • Hard to see milk level
  • Louder than the M5

Verdict: great features on paper. Until the durability reports settle down, buy it from a retailer with an easy return window.

eufy S1 and S1 Pro

  • HeatFlow warming (7 levels)
  • 7 suction levels
  • App control
  • Pro: charging case

eufy's pumps stand out for gentle warming during pumping, which some people find more comfortable. The S1 and S1 Pro share the same heated pump; the Pro adds a wireless charging case that recharges the pumps on the go (about 10 sessions per case charge, in one review). The S1 Pro has been one of the top-earning products in Amazon's whole baby category this year.

Two things to know: the hubs are large, so they don't suit every bra and show under fitted clothes, and positioning takes a few sessions to learn. Also read Amazon reviews critically. An independent 2026 analysis of one eufy pump listing rated many of its reviews as unreliable.

FAQ

What is the difference between the Momcozy M5 and M9?

Per Momcozy's own comparison, the M9 has 15 suction levels and up to 300 mmHg versus the M5's 9 levels and 280 mmHg, plus app control and separate left and right settings. The M5 is cheaper, quieter and simpler. Some M9 owners reported motor failures in early 2026, while the M5 has a longer track record.

What is the difference between the eufy S1 and S1 Pro?

The pump is essentially the same: both have eufy's HeatFlow warming and app control. The S1 Pro adds a wireless charging case that recharges the pumps on the go. The S1 costs less and suits people who can recharge between sessions.

Is the warming feature on eufy pumps worth it?

Some people find gentle warmth more comfortable. It is a comfort feature, not a proven way to increase milk supply. If supply is your concern, talk to an IBCLC lactation consultant.

Can I get these pumps through insurance?

Sometimes, usually as an upgrade where you pay the difference between your plan's rate and the pump's price. eufy sells through insurance directly, and suppliers like Aeroflow list which wearables your plan covers. Check before paying retail.
