About Little Nest Notes

Little Nest Notes helps expecting and new parents choose baby and postpartum gear without overspending or second-guessing safety.

I am {{AUTHOR_NAME}}. I write plain-English guides to the things new parents actually have to decide on: registries, breast pumps, monitors, strollers and the recovery essentials nobody tells you about.

How I research

  • Prices and specs come from the brand's or retailer's official pages, with the date I checked.
  • Every product is checked against the US Consumer Product Safety Commission recall list (cpsc.gov/Recalls) before it is recommended.
  • Sleep advice follows the American Academy of Pediatrics safe-sleep guidance. I do not recommend inclined sleepers, crib bumpers or weighted sleep products.
  • Downsides come from real parent feedback (Reddit, forums, independent reviews), not only the marketing page.
  • When I have used a product myself, I say so. When I have not, I say the guide is research-based.

What this site is not

I am not a doctor, nurse or lactation consultant, and nothing here is medical advice. For feeding, recovery, sleep or your baby's health, talk to your doctor, midwife, pediatrician or an IBCLC.
