What's worth buying on sale (and what isn't)

Worth it: big-ticket items you have already decided on, like a bottle washer, a monitor, a crib mattress, a stroller or a carrier. These rarely get cheaper than in the Black Friday and Prime Day windows.

Not worth it: anything on the regret list in the minimalist registry guide, such as bottle and wipe warmers, full bottle sets before baby tries them, or bulky swings. Also skip formula machines as a "deal" buy for now: a 2026 class-action lawsuit alleges some Baby Brezza Formula Pro machines under-dispense powder. Those are allegations, and the company says the suit has no merit, but there's no need to rush.

Registry tip: if your completion discount window is open, compare it with the sale price. They usually don't stack, so use whichever is lower.

FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, and Cyber Monday is November 30. Many baby brands start earlier: several 2026 sales run from mid-November into early December.

Is Black Friday a good time to buy baby gear?

Often, yes, for big-ticket items like bottle washers, monitors, crib mattresses and strollers, which are commonly 20 to 30% off. It is a good time to buy things already on your list, not a reason to buy extra gear.

Do registry completion discounts stack with Black Friday sales?

Usually not. Babylist's completion discount can't be combined with other offers, and Amazon and Target have their own terms. Compare the sale price with your completion discount and use whichever saves more.
