How these guides work

Every guide starts from official product pages and manuals, then adds what parents report on Reddit and in independent reviews. Everything recommended is checked against the US CPSC recall list, and sleep products follow American Academy of Pediatrics guidance: no inclined sleepers, loungers for sleep or weighted sleep sacks, ever.

This is product information, not medical advice. For feeding, recovery or your baby's health, ask your doctor, midwife, pediatrician or an IBCLC lactation consultant.
