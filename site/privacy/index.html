What is collected, and why

1. Hosting logs

The site is hosted by {{HOSTING_PROVIDER}}. Like every web host, it automatically records technical data when you load a page: IP address, browser type, the page requested, the referring page and the time. This is used only to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix errors, and is kept for the limited period set by the host. Legal basis (GDPR): legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)).

2. Visitor statistics (cookieless)

To see which guides are useful, the site can use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly tool that uses no cookies and stores no personal data. It counts page views, the referring site, country, device type and browser. Your IP address is not stored: Plausible turns it into a daily changing, anonymous code that is deleted after 24 hours, so you cannot be followed from day to day or across other sites. Plausible is an EU company and keeps its data in the EU. Legal basis: legitimate interest in understanding which content helps readers (Art. 6(1)(f)).

You can stop your visits being counted at any time: and choose “Don’t count my visits”. Blocking JavaScript or using a content blocker also works.

3. Contact form and email

If you use the contact form or email me, I receive your name, email address and message. I use them only to read and answer your message, and I delete them within 12 months unless we are still in touch. Form messages are delivered through the host's form service, which also checks them for spam (this can involve your IP address). Legal basis: your request and my legitimate interest in replying (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). Please don't send health details about you or your baby; I can't give medical advice anyway.

What is not collected

No accounts, no newsletter, no comments, no advertising or social-media tracking pixels, no fingerprinting and no third-party fonts. The site never asks for payment details: purchases happen on the retailers' own sites.

Cookies and your browser's storage

Little Nest Notes sets no cookies. When you close the privacy notice, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (key lnn-privacy-choice, plus plausible_ignore if you opt out of statistics). These stay on your device, are never sent to me, and you can clear them in your browser settings at any time.

Affiliate links

Some links go to retailers through affiliate programs (for example Amazon Associates, Awin, Impact, CJ and Rakuten). When you click one, you leave this site, and the retailer or network may set its own cookie on its site so that a purchase can be credited to Little Nest Notes. That happens under their privacy policies, not this one. I only see summary reports (for example "one bottle washer sold"), never your name, address or payment details.

Who else sees data

  • The web host ({{HOSTING_PROVIDER}}): hosting logs and contact-form messages, as a service provider (data processor) acting on my instructions.
  • Plausible Analytics: anonymous statistics only, if enabled.
  • My email provider: messages you send me.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, they rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. I do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are used in US state privacy laws (such as California's CCPA/CPRA), and I do not use it for targeted advertising. Browser privacy signals such as Global Privacy Control are respected automatically, because there is nothing to opt out of.
